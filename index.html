About

Analytical thinking.
Practical impact.

Hi, I’m Konstantina Gianniou. I’m a Data & Process Analyst who turns operational data into clear KPIs, dashboards and process improvements, using R, Tableau and Excel. Whether it was bark beetles, training gaps or false alarms, my work has had one thread: find the pattern in the data and fix what it points to.

I currently build AI-enabled business applications at Porcelana-Hatzigeorgiou.

Before that, I worked for over four years in operations and quality:

  • Majorel: owned quality for a 25-agent team, kept CSAT above 94%, and ran a Lean Six Sigma DMAIC project in R that cut identified training gaps by 30%.
  • Euroguard: as SOC Operations Manager, led 15 agents, built the company’s SOP knowledge base from scratch, used log diagnostics to find recurring false-positive patterns, and uncovered a €2k billing gap that had gone unnoticed for 6 months.

Alongside this, as a freelance statistical consultant and forest-research analyst, I built full analysis pipelines in R, MATLAB and SPSS for researchers and students, and presented findings at conferences in Greece and abroad.

I also build HTML tools for support and operations teams, such as knowledge bases, quiz builders and DMAIC trackers. They run entirely offline, so there’s no IT approval or data risk.

Portfolio and Etsy shop: see Digital tools and Selected work.

If you have a problem worth solving, reach out. I’m always glad to build the solution.
